About TravelGuide

We help travelers find destinations that actually fit them — not just the places everyone else is posting about.

Our Story

TravelGuide started in 2019 as a shared spreadsheet between three friends who kept sending each other the same "hidden gem" articles that turned out to be neither hidden nor gems. We got tired of recommendations written by people who'd never actually been to the places they were describing.

So we built something different: a small team of travelers who visit destinations ourselves, take notes on what's overhyped and what's genuinely worth the trip, and write it up honestly — including the parts that aren't glamorous.
